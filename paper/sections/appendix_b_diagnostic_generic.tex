\section{Method-Agnostic Validation of the Credal-Width Diagnostic}
\label{app:diagnostic-generic}

The bootstrap credal-width diagnostic measures whether bootstrap-resampled
probes can extract per-item information from the deferral target. To test
whether this diagnostic is specific to our gap mechanism, we compute it for a
Verma-style OVA learn-to-defer alternative and compare the predicted regime
against the observed selective-prediction performance.

\paragraph{Verma OVA-L2D alternative.}
We replace our expert-reliability supervision $y_{\text{expert}}(x)$ with the
original Verma-style construction $y_{\text{expert}}^{\text{Verma}} =
1-y_{\text{model}}$ on contrastive pairs. This supervision is perfectly
anti-correlated with the prediction-probe target by construction. The resulting
deferral probe therefore tests whether the two-head architecture alone is
responsible for the effect, or whether the dataset-specific
$y_{\text{expert}}$ signal carries additional per-item structure.

\paragraph{Hypothesis.}
If the credal-width diagnostic is method-agnostic, it should predict the
regime correctly for both deferral targets. On datasets where the diagnostic
predicts collapse for our gap, it should also predict collapse for the Verma
gap; on showcase datasets, both constructions should show wider bootstrap
intervals than the diagnostic regime.
