\section{Constrained-Optimization Deferral}
\label{app:constrained-deferral}

We can view binary commit/defer selection as a constrained linear program over
soft commit variables $\pi_i \in [0,1]$. The primary variant in the main text is
the $\Gamma$-maximin robust LP, which substitutes lower credal bounds
$\underline p_{\mathrm{pred}}$ and $\underline p_{\mathrm{amb}}$ into the
commit and defer costs. The appendix expected-cost variant uses point estimates
$\hat p_{\mathrm{pred}}$ and $\hat p_{\mathrm{amb}}$.

\paragraph{Expected-cost LP.}
For each calibration item $i$, the expected cost of committing is
$c_{\mathrm{wrong}}(1-\hat p_{\mathrm{pred},i})$ and the expected cost of
deferring is $c_{\mathrm{defer}}(1-\hat p_{\mathrm{amb},i})$. The Bayes variant
solves
\[
\begin{aligned}
\min_{\pi \in [0,1]^N}\quad
&\sum_i \pi_i c_{\mathrm{wrong}}(1-\hat p_{\mathrm{pred},i})
 +(1-\pi_i)c_{\mathrm{defer}}(1-\hat p_{\mathrm{amb},i}) \\
\mathrm{s.t.}\quad
&\kappa_{\min}N \le \sum_i \pi_i \le \kappa_{\max}N,\\
&\sum_i \pi_i(\hat p_{\mathrm{pred},i}-(1-\alpha)) \ge 0.
\end{aligned}
\]

\paragraph{Robust LP.}
The robust variant replaces $\hat p$ with lower bounds
$\underline p$. Because the objective and accuracy constraint are linear in the
probabilities, the worst case over interval credal sets is attained at the lower
endpoints. Thus the robust LP has the same form with
$\hat p_{\mathrm{pred}}, \hat p_{\mathrm{amb}}$ replaced by
$\underline p_{\mathrm{pred}}, \underline p_{\mathrm{amb}}$.

\paragraph{Relation to gap thresholding.}
When coverage-band constraints are inactive and credal intervals collapse to
points, the LP commits when the reduced cost of committing is below the reduced
cost of deferring. The conformal constraint contributes a dual variable
$\lambda$, yielding a per-item commit rule of the form
\[
c_{\mathrm{wrong}}(1-p_{\mathrm{pred}})
-\lambda(p_{\mathrm{pred}}-(1-\alpha))
<
c_{\mathrm{defer}}(1-p_{\mathrm{amb}}).
\]
This is a threshold rule in the two probe coordinates. The logit-space gap
$\Delta=\operatorname{logit}(p_{\mathrm{pred}})
-\operatorname{logit}(p_{\mathrm{amb}})$ is the simplest one-dimensional
ranking induced by these two coordinates; the LP generalizes it by allowing the
constraint duals and cost ratio to choose the operating point explicitly.

\paragraph{Credal intervals.}
For the ARR submission we use bootstrap probe intervals: train $K=20$
bootstrap-resampled probes for each head and take per-item lower, mean, and
upper probabilities. The lower bounds feed the robust LP; the means recover a
point-estimate comparison. Appendix sensitivity can vary $K$ and the bootstrap
regularization strength.
