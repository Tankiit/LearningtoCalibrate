\caption{\textbf{Independence implies underperformance.}
\textbf{(a)} Per-item joint distribution of probe outputs
$(p_{\mathrm{pred}}, p_{\mathrm{defer}})$ on MedQA / LLaMA.
The model-correctness probe $f_{\mathrm{pred}}$ separates items
along the horizontal axis (blue: correct; red: wrong), while
the expert-reliability probe $f_{\mathrm{defer}}$ does not
separate items along the vertical axis. The two probes encode
genuinely uncorrelated per-item information.
\textbf{(b)} AURC of the single-probe baseline $f_{\mathrm{pred}}$
and the gap composite $\Delta = \mathrm{logit}\,f_{\mathrm{pred}}
- \mathrm{logit}\,f_{\mathrm{defer}}$ on all $18$
non-constant-baseline cells ($15$ non-degenerate per-item +
$3$ TruthfulQA). Every line slopes upward: the gap is worse
than the single probe on every cell. Line color indicates the
BDD bootstrap-stability regime; tight BDD cells (blue) exhibit
the largest penalties, while borderline PubMedQA (orange)
exhibits the smallest. The two pre-deployment diagnostics
together predict not just \emph{whether} two-signal composition
will fail but also \emph{by how much}.}
