# ICLR 2027 — live results ledger

Updated 5 September 2026 from the current cached artifacts. This file is the
paper-facing source of truth for numbers and claim boundaries. Earlier prose
that treated verbalized confidence as an invariant measurement is superseded.

## Claim boundary and dataset scope

The stable contribution is the state channel: the fixed-order paired hidden
state feature improves selective prediction in all six cells on TruthfulQA and
all six cells on PopQA. The verbalized report is a protocol-conditioned
readout. Its results depend on the elicitation alphabet and legend; the
permuted-legend arm shows that item ordering is not invariant. Report numbers
therefore describe what the standard protocol produces, not a validated latent
measurement.

The report inventory is TruthfulQA plus Pavlick NLI because those are the six
cells with report caches. The state inventory is TruthfulQA plus PopQA because
those are the datasets with paired hidden-state caches. This inventory was
fixed by cache/design availability before interpreting the results.

### Letter11 under legend reversal

The fixed reversal is `A=100%, B=90%, ..., L=0%`, with the same letters and
same list positions. All eleven labels were injective single-token
continuations in all six cells, and all gates passed.

The primary `rho` is computed in **denoted-value space**: each arm's `Vdist`
is multiplied by that arm's mapped confidence levels before ranking.

| model | dataset | Spearman rho | 95% item interval | v1 reliability | result |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Llama | TruthfulQA | .020 | [-.048, .093] | .351 | non-invariant |
| Llama | Pavlick NLI | -.004 | [-.093, .086] | .268 | non-invariant |
| Mistral | TruthfulQA | -.026 | [-.095, .045] | .365 | non-invariant |
| Mistral | Pavlick NLI | .055 | [-.032, .138] | .866 | non-invariant |
| Qwen | TruthfulQA | .067 | [-.006, .134] | .468 | non-invariant |
| Qwen | Pavlick NLI | -.054 | [-.145, .035] | .207 | non-invariant |

This is a six-of-six failure of item-ordering invariance **under legend
reversal**. Pure value tracking predicts `+1`; an unchanged token distribution
under reversal predicts `-1`. Neither predicts the observed near-zero values.
The arm does not separate glyph from legend position, because the reversal
keeps both fixed. A shuffled-listing arm would be needed for that separation.

Llama numeric controls are `.237 → .010` on TruthfulQA and `.239 → .005` on
Pavlick for v1 versus permuted agreement with numeric101.

### Superseded partition test

The exploratory seed-0 TRAIN-fold comparison is retained only for provenance:
cross-format Spearman `.205`, letter reliability `.261`, numeric reliability
`.791`, ceiling `.454`, ratio `.452`, bootstrap interval `[.262, .647]`.
Because the interval crosses the frozen `.50` threshold, this is supporting
evidence only and is superseded by the same-instrument reversal arm.

## Report-distribution diagnostics and robustness

Mean normalized entropy of cached `Vdist_pos` under the 11-value alphabet:

| model | TruthfulQA | Pavlick NLI | mean V⁺ TruthfulQA | mean V⁺ Pavlick |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Llama | .888 | .932 | .656 | .595 |
| Mistral | .922 | .923 | .512 | .526 |
| Qwen | .152 | .291 | .895 | .922 |

Entropy describes within-item diffuseness; it is not an across-item ranking or
an AURC result. Rewording the confidence question changes the paired-gap
readout by more than the original mean absolute gap in several cells (ratios
1.02–2.08 on TruthfulQA and 1.05–7.58 on Pavlick). This is prompt sensitivity
of the protocol, not proof that no report signal exists.

## Limitations and withdrawn claims

- The report arm is teacher-forced and uses one dataset-supplied `a⁻` per item;
  the fixed partition is not an independent confirmatory fold.
- `S_conf`/exact confidence-writer provenance is unresolved. Positional claims
  are limited to the verified question-boundary `s_q` and paired hidden-state
  metadata.
- Do not generalize the reversal result to arbitrary permutations; always say
  “under legend reversal.”
- Do not call the verbal report a stable measurement or say it simply
  “does/does not help” without naming the protocol and measurability status.
- Do not use “glyph tracking,” “coherence,” “different functions,”
  “non-discriminating,” or a `0/6` verbalized-confidence null as paper claims.
- The target-selected `h_a = where(y, h_pos, h_neg)` direction geometry and its
  permutation/reliability interpretation are withdrawn.
