# Decomposing the Llama/TruthfulQA alphabet flip

Generated by `python verbalized_conformal_confidence/alphabet_flip_analysis.py`
from the canonical caches plus the two `fine_conf.pt` numeric caches. Positive
`delta` favors the report channel; `flip` is always `AURC_letter - AURC_numeric`.
Per-check CSVs, per-item rows and decision metadata sit beside this file in
`manifest.json`.

**Status: exploratory.** This is a decomposition of an already-observed flip.
`s_q` is always fitted on the fold complementary to the one scored, and both
folds are reported. Nothing here opens a confirmatory count.

## Precondition, answered

Numeric caches with full `Vdist` exist for `llama3_8b/truthfulqa` and
`llama3_8b/pavlick_nli` and for no other cell. So (1), (3), (4), (5) and (6) are
free on both llama cells; (2) is free on those two and needs a fresh elicitation
pass for the four mistral/qwen cells. See `cache_inventory.csv`.

## Naming correction, and the confound it exposes

The cached numeric run is `digits101-v1` — levels 0..100 in steps of 1, no
legend, "Answer with a number from 0 to 100" — not a `numeric11`. The contrast
that produced the flip therefore moves three things at once:

| | letter11 | digits101 |
|---|---|---|
| glyph | `A..L` | digit strings |
| legend | in-context `A=0% ... L=100%` | none; the token is the value |
| resolution | K = 11 | K = 101 |

(4) removes resolution post hoc. Nothing offline separates glyph from legend.

## Result-changing findings

- **The flip is real and much better resolved than the two intervals suggest.**
  `-0.038 +/- 0.023` and `+0.079 +/- 0.023` share items and seeds, and the
  contrast `(A_q - A_numeric) - (A_q - A_letter)` equals `A_letter - A_numeric`
  exactly — `s_q` cancels, so nearly all of each arm's `+/- 0.023` is baseline
  variance common to both. Paired: **+0.1175, seed SD 0.0038** (84% of the
  per-arm SD removed), item bootstrap 95% CI **[0.088, 0.144]**. Report it
  paired; two overlapping-looking intervals invite the wrong conclusion.

- **V_pos alone carries the flip, so this reaches the deployable object.**
  `A_letter - A_numeric` on TruthfulQA: V_pos **+0.120**, gap **+0.118**,
  `-V_neg` +0.084. The effect is not specific to the paired construction — it is
  about verbalized confidence generally, and V+ on a single answer is the
  deployed score. This belongs in Sec. 1, not in a robustness paragraph.

- **It is not a resolution failure.** Re-reading the *same* 101-level
  distributions restricted to the letter alphabet's own 11-level support
  (restrict-and-renormalize, exactly what the letter readout does) retains
  **99%** of the flip (+0.1165 of +0.1175). Coarsening the numeric scalar onto
  the same grid retains 93%. Matching K does not remove the effect, so
  "the letter alphabet just has fewer levels" is not the explanation.

- **The letter elicitation does not concentrate.** Under letters the per-item
  distribution is near-uniform over the legend — `H_norm` 0.888, effective
  support **8.5 of 11 levels** — which pulls every item's V toward the
  alphabet's midpoint: across-item SD of the gap 0.060 versus 0.374 under
  numeric, and V_pos confined to a 5th-95th percentile range of [0.57, 0.76]
  against [0.20, 0.94] under numeric (see `scatter_llama3_8b_truthfulqa.png`). Under numeric the distribution is peaked:
  `H_norm` 0.485, effective support 10.9 of 101.

- **The legend costs the answer signal.** `rho(V_pos, V_neg)` is **0.610** under
  letters and **0.026** under numeric (paired item-bootstrap difference
  **-0.583, CI [-0.672, -0.495]**). Under letters the coupling between the two
  sides *exceeds* the instrument's own alternate-form reliability (0.401 on the
  positive side, ratio 1.52): the two candidates agree with each other more than
  either agrees with its own reworded self. Under numeric reliability is 0.796
  and the sides are independent. That is answer-invariance under letters and
  answer-discrimination under numeric.

- **The two alphabets are not the same measurement, even after correcting for
  unreliability.** `rho(V_letter, V_numeric)` on TruthfulQA is 0.185 (V_pos),
  0.326 (V_neg), 0.528 (gap). Dividing by the reliability ceiling
  `sqrt(rel_letter * rel_numeric)` gives **0.33 (V_pos)**, 0.49 (V_neg), 0.73
  (gap). The paired gap is close to a shared construct; the individual sides are
  not. A low raw correlation between two noisy instruments is not evidence of
  disagreement — a disattenuated 0.33 is.

- **`llama3_8b/pavlick_nli` was excluded for a property of the format, not of
  the model.** The same fixed gate (5pp bins anchored at zero, exponentiated bin
  entropy, threshold 2) gives **1.068 effective bins under letters** (98.8% of
  mass in one bin, 2.4% of item pairs separated at gate resolution) and **3.027
  under numeric** (46.6% max bin, 59.6% of pairs separated). The exclusion stays
  correct as applied — the gate is computed on the score actually used — but
  Sec. 5 must say "under the letter alphabet this cell yields no usable
  ordering", not "this model's report is degenerate on this dataset". Under the
  format that *is* readable, the cell does not improve, and strongly so
  (`-0.378 +/- 0.049`); the rewrite does not rescue the report channel there.

## Findings that bound the above

- **The flip is distribution-wide, not a tail.** Removing the 20 test items with
  the largest leave-one-out influence on the AURC difference moves the flip from
  0.118 to about 0.10, an excess over removing 20 items at random of only
  0.01–0.03. The top 5% of items carry 22–25% of the total absolute influence —
  mild concentration, roughly 4x uniform, not twenty items doing the work. A
  general statement about elicitation format is supported.

- **Direction is not uniform across datasets.** On `pavlick_nli` the flip runs
  the other way (`-0.304`, CI [-0.341, -0.260]) — numeric is much worse there.
  The claim that survives both cells is "the alphabet is result-bearing", not
  "the numeric alphabet is better".

- **`V_neg` read in its raw direction gets worse under numeric** (flip -0.068)
  while `-V_neg` improves (+0.084). AURC is direction-sensitive; both are
  reported so the sign cannot be misread as a finding.

## Still open

- **Shuffled legend.** Permuting `A=70%, B=10%, ...` while holding the letters
  fixed separates "V tracks the glyph" from "V tracks the value it denotes". Not
  computable offline — the legend is inside the prompt, so a permutation is a
  new forward pass.
- **Single-digit alphabet (`digit10-direct`), 0-9 mapped to 0-90%.** Single
  tokens under both grouping and splitting tokenizers, no legend. It is the only
  way to separate the alphabet effect from the tokenization effect — currently
  "numeric vs letter" is entangled with "contaminated vs clean" and exactly one
  model is interpretable. One elicitation pass; TruthfulQA alone is 817 items x
  3 models x 2 sides. Three things to state precisely when this reaches text:
  - **K is held approximately, not matched.** K = 10 against the letter arm's
    11. That the one-level gap does not carry the effect rests on the
    support-restriction result above (99% retained on Llama) — an argument, not
    a matched control.
  - **Glyph and mapping stay entangled.** `digit10-direct` against `letter11`
    varies both together, exactly as `digits101` did. The run establishes
    whether the effect exists beyond Llama; it does not say what the effect is
    made of. Characterisation needs the permuted-legend arm, which holds glyph
    fixed and moves mapping alone.
  - **The outcome is not presumed.** An effect on Llama with nulls on Mistral
    and Qwen is live, and yields a narrower claim — one model, format-sensitive
    — not a broader one. The decision rule mapping each outcome onto a claim has
    to be fixed before the look, in the house style of
    `paper/PREREGISTRATION_ADDITIVITY.md`. **No prereg exists for this arm yet.**
