# Cached no-GPU checks

> **Standing correction (2026-09-01):** the across-position `d_q`/`d_a`
> analysis below is withdrawn because `h_a = where(y, h_pos, h_neg)` uses the
> target to select the input branch. Its permutation null and split-half ceiling
> inherit that defect. Do not cite or regenerate the alignment claim. See
> [`paper/session_2026-09-01.md`](../../paper/session_2026-09-01.md). The
> fixed-order paired-feature AURC results are unaffected.

Generated by `python scripts/cached_claim_checks.py` from the six cells that contain
`Vdist_pos`: Llama 3 8B, Mistral 7B, and Qwen 2.5 7B on TruthfulQA and Pavlick NLI.
The outcome throughout is the cached mean-token-logprob preference
`logprob_pos > logprob_neg`. Positive delta AURC means improvement over the held-out
question-only probe `s_q`.

## 1. Alternate-prompt audit

`Valt_neg` exists alongside `Valt_pos` in all six cells. Pavlick NLI has alternate-prompt
values for all 496 items; TruthfulQA has them for 500 of 817 items. The paired gap-noise
denominator is
`abs((V_pos - V_neg) - (Valt_pos - Valt_neg))`. Its mean is 3.44–12.38 times the
absolute mean answer effect across cells. Correlations between the two sides' signed
perturbations range from -0.086 to 0.685. Wording shifts often have the same sign, but do
not cancel reliably enough in magnitude to support the stronger paraphrase-robust claim.
For Qwen/TruthfulQA specifically, the gap-noise ratio is 4.98 against the signed mean
effect (1.01 against the mean absolute gap). On the identical alternate-prompt subset,
delta AURC drops from +0.110 to +0.032 and is positive in only two of three seeds. The
original +0.109 cell is therefore prompt-sensitive and cannot be described as
unconditionally surviving rewording.

## 2. Vdist entropy

All cached cells use the same 11-value numeric alphabet, 0 through 100 in steps of 10.
`vdist_entropy_by_alphabet.csv` reports per-cell mean normalized entropy
`H(Vdist_pos) / log(11)` and mean `V_pos`. Llama and Mistral have diffuse distributions
(`H_norm` 0.888–0.932); Qwen is much sharper (0.152–0.291) and has much higher mean
`V_pos` (0.895–0.922). Qwen also places about 99.8% probability mass on the allowed
alphabet. Llama places 85–94% and Mistral 61–86%, so high entropy is not solely an
off-alphabet syntax failure: their normalized restricted readout is itself diffuse.

## 3. Survives table

Across the six cells, the mean delta AURC and number of positive cells are:

| signal | mean delta AURC | positive cells |
|---|---:|---:|
| logprob margin | +0.1524 | 6/6 |
| hidden-state answer probe | +0.0452 | 6/6 |
| `V_pos - V_neg` | -0.0219 | 1/6 (Qwen/TruthfulQA) |
| `V_pos` (teacher-forced verbalized confidence) | -0.0601 | 1/6 |
| `ntok_pos` length null | -0.0860 | 1/6 (Llama/Pavlick) |

The only positive `V_pos - V_neg` cell is Qwen/TruthfulQA. Per-item `Vdist`
entropy and across-item ranking adequacy must not be conflated: diffuse
restricted distributions describe within-item uncertainty, whereas AURC uses
only the ordering of the across-item gap. Under the outcome-free rule in
`paper/MEASUREMENT_ADEQUACY.md`, only Llama/Pavlick is not measurable; the other
five cells induce adequate gap rankings. The result is therefore one positive
cell, four non-improving measurable cells, and one non-measurable cell—not a
six-cell null.

The logprob-margin row is an oracle upper bound, not an independent predictor: the outcome
is defined by the sign of that same margin. The `ntok` result is dataset-sensitive: it is
positive for Llama/Pavlick but negative in four cells, so the per-cell table must accompany
the aggregate.

### Legend invariance

The fixed reversed `letter11-permuted` arm is a same-glyph, same-position
re-encoding test. In all six cells, the primary Spearman correlation between
`V⁺_v1` and `V⁺_perm` is near zero (0.020, -0.004, -0.026, 0.055, 0.067,
-0.054). These correlations are computed in each arm's **denoted-value
space**: each cached `Vdist` is multiplied by that arm's mapped confidence
levels before ranking. The result is therefore a six-of-six failure of item
ordering invariance to the legend. It does not identify a replacement
mechanism: neither pure value tracking (`rho=+1`) nor unchanged-token tracking
under reversal (`rho=-1`) predicts the observed near-zero agreement. See
[`letter11_permuted/README.md`](letter11_permuted/README.md).

## 4. Across-position direction cosine

`d_q` classifies the cached outcome from `h_q`. For the paired post-answer representation,
each item uses the state following the answer the model actually prefers: `h_pos` when the
outcome is correct and `h_neg` otherwise. Thus `d_q` and `d_a` classify the same outcome on
the same items at pre- and post-answer positions.

The balanced TruthfulQA cosines are 0.258 (Llama), 0.419 (Mistral), and 0.229 (Qwen), all
outside their 1,000-permutation nulls (`p=0.001`). Their split-half attenuation ceilings are
0.434, 0.602, and 0.554, so the observed alignments reach 59%, 70%, and 41% of ceiling.
This is evidence for a shared component, strongest for Mistral, rather than identity of the
pre- and post-answer computations. Pavlick NLI is weaker: observed/ceiling is 0.327/0.972,
0.111/0.937, and 0.075/0.966. Split-half ceilings are `sqrt(r_q * r_a)` and the two component
reliabilities are retained in the CSV.

Only the upstream default `balance=True` rows are reported. In this two-centroid estimator,
`balance=False` changes only the positive scalar multiplying the centroid difference and cannot
change its cosine.

The high Pavlick ceilings were checked against the direct answer-identity diagnostic
`cos(d_a, mean(h_pos - h_neg))`. The Pavlick values are 0.212, 0.799, and 0.577, versus
TruthfulQA's 0.727, 0.642, and 0.750. Pavlick's directions are unusually homogeneous, but
literal verbalizer-token identity is not a consistent explanation across models. The ceiling
contrast is supported; that particular causal explanation remains a hypothesis.
