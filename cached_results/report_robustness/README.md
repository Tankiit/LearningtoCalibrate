# Report-channel robustness checks

Generated by `python verbalized_conformal_confidence/report_channel_robustness.py`
from the canonical caches. Positive deltas favor the report channel. The full
per-seed rows and compact summaries are retained beside this file; thresholds
and decision metadata are in `manifest.json`.

## Result-changing findings

- **Alphabet choice is result-bearing.** On Llama/TruthfulQA, the report delta
  changes from `-0.038 +/- 0.023` with the 11-level letter elicitation to
  `+0.079 +/- 0.023` with the clean 101-level numeric elicitation. On
  Llama/Pavlick, the letter score is not measurable (`N_eff=1.068`), while the
  numeric score is measurable and does not improve (`-0.378 +/- 0.049`). These
  are different elicitation alphabets and resolutions, so the result must be
  stated per alphabet; the cache does not isolate alphabet symbols from
  granularity.
- **The value-weighted mean is not fully readout-invariant.** The high-mass
  functional changes Qwen/Pavlick from `-0.0065 +/- 0.0152` to
  `+0.0028 +/- 0.0081`. This crosses the predeclared zero boundary but is small
  relative to seed variation. Qwen/TruthfulQA improves under every measurable
  readout; the Llama/TruthfulQA and Mistral cells do not improve under every
  measurable readout. The paper should disclose the Qwen/Pavlick exception and
  not call the null readout-independent.
- **Compliance stratification does not globally close the conditioning
  objection.** Mistral/TruthfulQA has no items with both-side alphabet mass at
  least 0.90; its middle stratum has only about 34 test items per seed and flips
  positive. Mistral/Pavlick's high stratum also flips positive but has only
  about 12 test items per seed. Llama/TruthfulQA remains negative in its high
  stratum, and both Qwen cells are wholly high-compliance and retain their
  original outcomes. Compliance therefore supports a bounded, per-cell claim,
  not a universal closure.

## Findings that do not change the main cell outcomes

- **Estimator parity:** the learned probe on `[V+, V-, V+-V-]` is never at
  least 0.01 better than the fixed gap. It differs by at most 0.004 in five
  cells and is 0.023 worse on Mistral/Pavlick. Under the exact predeclared rule,
  this is `mixed_review_required` rather than `functional_not_binding`, because
  the latter required every cell to fall within 0.01. Crucially, there is no
  evidence that the fixed estimator suppressed report information.
- **Target convention:** the measurable-cell outcomes are unchanged under the
  sum-logprob target. The cached mean/sum label agreement is **0.851--0.972 by
  cell**, not the proposed 0.93--0.94; manuscript text must use the cached
  values. Llama/Pavlick crosses zero under the sum target but remains
  non-measurable under the fixed score-only gate.
- **Metric choice:** every measurable cell has the same improve/does-not-improve
  label under AURC and AUROC. The Brier sensitivity also preserves the labels,
  but it affinely maps the signed report gap to `[0,1]` and compares an
  uncalibrated quantity with a fitted probability, so it is not a like-for-like
  primary metric.

The positive Qwen/TruthfulQA cell remains notably less stable than the state
gaps: `+0.109 +/- 0.046`, versus state-gap seed standard deviations in the
roughly `0.004--0.018` range. That asymmetry should be reported rather than
hidden by the seed mean.

## Construction language that must remain in the paper

The cache contains one `h_neg` vector per question (`[N,d]`, not `[N,K,d]`). A
distractor sweep therefore requires a second extraction, and the current result
is conditional on one negative-answer construction. TruthfulQA's curated
falsehood and Pavlick NLI's complementary label agree in direction, but that is
weaker than robustness to the negative answer.

The negative answer is dataset-supplied rather than model-generated. The
deployed `V+` score on the model's own answer performs worse than the paired
report (`-0.060` versus `-0.011` over measurable cells), so this limitation
points away from the paper's conclusion rather than rescuing the report
channel.

Teacher-forcing the negative answer is off-policy. It buys exact item-level
difficulty control at the cost of visiting states that sampling may not reach;
the observed alphabet-mass range is evidence of that cost. Pair order is fixed
by gold identity before the target is computed and both channels receive both
ordered branches. This differs from the withdrawn target-selected `h_a`
construction. Finally, the study has one fixed partition and no independent
confirmatory fold.

## Baseline defense

The question-only baseline is not artificially strong: on identical
TruthfulQA items, the paired state probe improves its AURC from 0.481 to 0.335
for Llama, 0.402 to 0.298 for Mistral, and 0.414 to 0.260 for Qwen.
