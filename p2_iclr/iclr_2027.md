# AISTATS 2027 — live results ledger

Updated 9 September 2026 from the current cached artifacts. This file is the
paper-facing source of truth for numbers and claim boundaries. Earlier prose
that treated verbalized confidence as an invariant measurement is superseded.

## Claim boundary and dataset scope

The stable contribution is the state channel: the fixed-order paired hidden
state feature improves selective prediction in all six cells on TruthfulQA and
all six cells on PopQA. The verbalized report is a protocol-conditioned
readout. Its results depend on the elicitation alphabet and legend; the
permuted-legend arm was intended to test item-order invariance, but its legacy
artifact is withdrawn from inference; a fresh provenance-controlled paired
extraction now supplies the raw reversal result. Report numbers therefore
describe what the standard protocol produces, not a validated latent
measurement.

**Status amendment — 8 September 2026.** The legacy reversed-legend artifacts
have no recoverable schema, read-position, padding, checkpoint, or
extractor-version provenance. A v3 regression against the current runner failed
at `max_abs(Vdist)=0.988` on the first cell. A controlled same-process check on
64 Llama/TruthfulQA items found `common_prefix_ids=[]`; the legacy and current
readout paths produced identical distributions, yet both differed from the
saved artifact (median max deviation `.244`, maximum `.968`, 28.1% above `.5`).
The mismatch is therefore not explained by the prefix or padding/read-position
path. It remains an unresolved model/checkpoint/tokenizer or
artifact-generation provenance mismatch for the legacy file. The legacy
reversed-legend result is withdrawn from inference: its near-zero
correlations, six-of-six count, selection instability, and figure footer are
not live claims. The fresh rerun is the live provenance-controlled result;
its pre-registered reliability comparison is applied below.

The body report inventory is TruthfulQA. Pavlick NLI is retained as a
label-word control in Appendix Y: its answer span is a single relation label,
so the confidence report is a report about a verbalizer token rather than a
free-text answer. The state inventory is TruthfulQA plus PopQA because those
are the datasets with paired hidden-state caches; PopQA has no matched report
elicitation cache because confidence was not elicited under the locked report
protocol. This inventory was fixed by cache/design availability before
interpreting the results.

### Fixed analysis conventions

The primary state representation is the span-mean hidden state over the
answer tokens. First-token pooling is the robustness comparison. Table 2 and
Figure 1A therefore use span-mean as the primary column and report first-token
pooling second.

PopQA has no report elicitation because that pass was outside the locked
compute/design scope; PopQA is a state-only extension, not a missing report
cell.

## Format and legend results

The elicitation vocabulary has three separable objects: the value set, the
token set, and the mapping from tokens to values. The reversal arm perturbs
the mapping while holding the value and token sets fixed. A token-substitution
arm would hold values and positions fixed while changing one token, testing
whether mass follows the denoted value or the token. A shuffled-listing arm
would hold the mapping fixed while changing list position, testing positional
effects. Only reversal is currently run; substitution and shuffled listing are
open follow-up arms and are not used as explanations of the reversal result.
The substitution arm is preregistered in
[`p2_substitution_preregistration.md`](prereg/p2_substitution_preregistration.md);
its per-letter neutral-prior gate is not yet complete.

In the canonical Llama/TruthfulQA `Vdist_pos` cache, the highest aggregate
forward mass is on `H`: mean mass `.179669` (median winner also `H`). This is a
cache diagnostic, not a post-hoc substitution result; selecting `H` and a
replacement token is frozen in
[`p2_substitution_preregistration.md`](prereg/p2_substitution_preregistration.md);
the substitution forward pass remains blocked until its neutral-prior and
all-tokenizer gates pass.

On Llama/TruthfulQA, `AURC_letter − AURC_numeric` is +.120 for `V⁺` and
+.118 for the gap, with paired gap CI `[.088, .144]`. Numeric is not a
universal-improvement claim. Pavlick format contrasts are not body evidence
and remain part of the label-word control record.

Within-format full-set correlations for `V⁺,V⁻` are:

| format | Pearson | Spearman |
|---|---:|---:|
| letter11 | .610 | .657 |
| numeric101 | .026 | .022 |

Cross-format Llama/TruthfulQA agreement is Spearman `.237` (Pearson `.185`).
Pavlick agreement is retained with the label-word control rather than used in
the body format claim.

### Letter11 under legend reversal — legacy audit record (withdrawn)

The following table is retained for audit only and must not be cited as a live
result. The legacy artifact failed provenance validation; the fresh paired
extraction below is the only reversal result used for inference. The legacy
artifact's internal consistency does not repair its missing provenance.

The fixed reversal is `A=100%, B=90%, ..., L=0%`, with the same letters and
same list positions. All eleven labels were injective single-token
continuations in all six cells, and all gates passed.

The primary `rho` is computed in **denoted-value space**: each arm's `Vdist`
is multiplied by that arm's mapped confidence levels before ranking.

| model | dataset | Spearman rho | 95% item interval | v1 reliability | result |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Llama | TruthfulQA | .020 | [-.048, .093] | .351 | non-invariant |
| Llama | Pavlick NLI | -.004 | [-.093, .086] | .268 | non-invariant |
| Mistral | TruthfulQA | -.026 | [-.095, .045] | .365 | non-invariant |
| Mistral | Pavlick NLI | .055 | [-.032, .138] | .866 | non-invariant |
| Qwen | TruthfulQA | .067 | [-.006, .134] | .468 | non-invariant |
| Qwen | Pavlick NLI | -.054 | [-.145, .035] | .207 | non-invariant |

These provisional values would be a six-of-six failure of item-ordering
invariance **under legend reversal** if reproduced. Pure value tracking
predicts `+1`; an unchanged token distribution under reversal predicts `-1`.
Neither predicts the observed near-zero values. The arm does not separate glyph
from legend position, because the reversal keeps both fixed. A shuffled-listing
arm would be needed for that separation.

The legacy Llama numeric controls are `.237 → .010` on TruthfulQA and
`.239 → .005` on Pavlick for v1 versus permuted agreement with numeric101;
these are also provisional because they use the unreproduced reversed arm.

### Provenance-controlled rerun — raw result

The legacy values above are withdrawn from inference. A fresh paired extraction
reran both the forward and reversed legends under pinned model revisions and a
pinned package environment, without reading the legacy artifact. The forward
arm reproduces the canonical forward-cache ordering with Spearman rho `.9991`
to `.9999` across the six cells; all six cells pass the `.10` mass gate with
usable fraction `1.000`.

| model | dataset | n | `n_alt` | fresh Spearman rho | paired item-bootstrap 95% interval | `rel_fwd` | frozen-rule result |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Llama | TruthfulQA | 817 | 500 | .123 | [.048, .196] | .351 | rejects |
| Llama | Pavlick NLI | 496 | 496 | .169 | [.081, .250] | .268 | rejects |
| Mistral | TruthfulQA | 817 | 500 | .058 | [-.009, .122] | .365 | rejects |
| Mistral | Pavlick NLI | 496 | 496 | .782 | [.751, .808] | .866 | rejects |
| Qwen | TruthfulQA | 817 | 500 | .340 | [.276, .404] | .468 | rejects |
| Qwen | Pavlick NLI | 496 | 496 | .125 | [.032, .214] | .208 | indeterminate |

These are provenance-controlled raw correlations. The intervals are paired
item bootstraps over the same forward/reversed item draws (`B=2000`, seed
`20260908`); `n_alt` is the number of canonical forward items with an
alternate-form `Valt_pos` reliability observation. The ID-aligned
alternate-form `Valt_pos` reference is the canonical `rel_fwd` column. Under
the frozen rule, the three TruthfulQA intervals and two Pavlick intervals lie
below their references; Qwen/Pavlick is indeterminate because its interval
contains `rel_fwd`. This supports the narrower statement that reversal
preserves only part of the ordering, with the retained amount cell-dependent;
it does not support the word “removes” or a mechanism claim. Artifacts and
metadata are recorded in
[`cached_results/letter11_provenance_v1/README.md`](../cached_results/letter11_provenance_v1/README.md).

## Limitations and withdrawn claims

## Corrections register

- The legacy reversed-legend result (`rho` near zero, six of six) was
  superseded on 8 September 2026 by the provenance-controlled rerun; the live
  range is `.058–.782`, with paired item-bootstrap intervals recorded above.
- Pavlick NLI was demoted on 9 September 2026 to a label-word control after
  the reversal result was reviewed. The reason is design-based—the answer span
  is a single relation label—but the demotion decision followed that result.

The report-channel body therefore uses the three TruthfulQA cells. Pavlick's
cached results remain available in Appendix Y and are not deleted.

- The report arm is teacher-forced and uses one dataset-supplied `a⁻` per item;
  the fixed partition is not an independent confirmatory fold.
- `S_conf`/exact confidence-writer provenance is unresolved. Positional claims
  are limited to the verified question-boundary `s_q` and paired hidden-state
  metadata.
- Do not generalize the reversal result to arbitrary permutations; always say
  “under legend reversal.”
- Do not call the verbal report a stable measurement or say it simply
  “does/does not help” without naming the protocol and measurability status.
- Do not use “glyph tracking,” “coherence,” “different functions,”
  “non-discriminating,” or a `0/6` verbalized-confidence null as paper claims.
- The target-selected `h_a = where(y, h_pos, h_neg)` direction geometry and its
  permutation/reliability interpretation are withdrawn.
